# Data validation report (S1-S2)

Stage 0 feasibility experiment, shared 6D motion transformer.
Generated by `scripts/s1_ingest.py` and `scripts/s2_extract.py`.
Source project `../gaga_jcvpca` was read only; nothing in it was modified.

---

## 1. What was ingested

24 Motive solved-skeleton CSV exports, 5.51 GB, SHA-256 recorded for each in
`data/immutable/INPUT_MANIFEST.json`. Four participants x three timepoints x
two repetitions, complete with no missing recordings.

All timing and identity metadata is keyed on the **embedded Motive
`Capture Start Time`**, never on filenames.

### 1.1 Filenames disagree with capture time

| Recording | Filename date | Embedded capture date |
|---|---|---|
| `252_T2_P1_R1` | 2026-04-26 | **2026-05-19** |
| `252_T2_P1_R2` | 2026-04-26 | **2026-05-19** |

This is not cosmetic. On filenames alone, participant 252's T2 (April 26)
appears to precede its T1 (April 28), which would invert the longitudinal
contrast for that participant. Using embedded capture time, ordering is
monotonic for all four participants.

### 1.2 Longitudinal spacing

| Participant | T1 to T2 | T2 to T3 | T1 to T3 | Ordering |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 252 | 21 d | 20 d | **42 d** | monotonic |
| 651 | 10 d | 16 d | 27 d | monotonic |
| 671 | 9 d | 19 d | 28 d | monotonic |
| 790 | 11 d | 17 d | 28 d | monotonic |

Participant 252 was followed for 42 days against roughly 28 for the others, a
50 percent longer window. This is carried forward as a pre-specified
**protocol-duration sensitivity**, not as grounds for exclusion.

### 1.3 Repetitions are genuinely within-session

R1 and R2 are separated by 8.9 to 15.0 minutes and always fall on the same
calendar day. The R1-R2 contrast therefore measures **within-session
repetition variability**, which is the intended reference quantity. It is not
a general test-retest noise floor and must not be described as one.

### 1.4 Length units are not uniform

`252_T3_P1_R1` and `252_T3_P1_R2` were exported in **Meters**; the other 22
recordings are in Millimeters. Units are recorded per recording and asserted.

This does not affect any result in this project, because the entire pipeline
operates on bone **orientations**, which are unitless. It is recorded because
any future positional, bone-length or amplitude check must convert first. Note
also that `skeleton_marker_to_meters` in the source project applies an axis
permutation only on the Millimeters branch, so a naive reuse of that helper on
`252-T3` would silently produce differently oriented positions.

### 1.5 One session is shared between participants

2026-01-15 contains both `651-T1` and `671-T2`. Any session-level artifact on
that day lands on different timepoints for the two participants, which is
mildly reassuring rather than a confound.

## 2. The skeleton template changes mid-study

This was not anticipated and is the most consequential finding of S1-S2.

| Participant | T1 | T2 | T3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 252 | 55-bone | 55-bone | 55-bone |
| 651 | **51-bone** | 55-bone | 55-bone |
| 671 | **51-bone** | **51-bone** | 55-bone |
| 790 | 55-bone | 55-bone | 55-bone |

The 55-bone template carries `Spine2`, `Spine3`, `Spine4` and `Neck2`; the
51-bone template does not. Participants 651 and 671 switch templates partway
through the study, 651 between T1 and T2 and 671 between T2 and T3.

The root bone is renamed at the same time: `651:651` becomes `651_T2:651_T2`
and `671:671` becomes `T3_671:T3_671`.

**Why this matters.** Any pipeline that fixes a link map per participant will
have that map change meaning at exactly the timepoint boundary the study is
trying to measure. A template artifact would then be perfectly confounded with
longitudinal change. In the source project this has three visible consequences
on the affected recordings:

* the three pelvis links are dropped entirely, because the cached column names
  are derived from the literal root bone name, which changed;
* `Ab_to_Chest` is replaced by the four-segment spine chain;
* `Neck_to_Head` is populated with the `Neck2 -> Head` rotation instead
  (see 3.2).

**Why this project is unaffected.** The 18 canonical links are defined by
anatomical endpoints and resolved against each recording's own hierarchy, so
the same 18 rotations are produced from all 24 recordings regardless of
template. This was chosen for topology reconciliation across participants; it
turns out to also be necessary *within* participants.

## 3. Extraction validation

For every link, `q_rel = inv(q_parent_global) * q_child_global`, then
quaternion sign continuity, then the SO(3) log map, then a zero-phase 4th-order
Butterworth low pass at 10 Hz applied in the tangent space. Extraction always
precedes filtering.

### 3.1 Agreement with the trusted source cache

384 link-recordings could be compared against `gaga_jcvpca` cached rotations.

| Outcome | Count |
|---|---|
| Exact agreement, 0.000e+00 rad | **378** |
| Explained by a documented source-side alias | 6 |
| Unexplained disagreement | **0** |

Agreement is bit-identical, not merely within tolerance. This validates the
rotation convention, multiplication order, axis handling, sign-continuity
treatment and filter design all at once. It also confirms that the vectorised
sign-continuity implementation here is equivalent to the sequential loop in the
source project.

### 3.2 The six alias cases

All six are `Neck_to_Head`, in the recordings that carry a `Neck2` bone under a
participant the source project treats as 51-bone: `651-T2` (both reps),
`651-T3` (both reps) and `671-T3` (both reps).

The source project stores only the **final** sub-link under the canonical name,
so its `Neck_to_Head` column actually holds `Neck2 -> Head`. Reproducing that
alias here matches the cache to **0.000e+00 rad**, which proves the discrepancy
is a source-side labelling choice and not an extraction error.

The anatomical cost of the alias is not small: the true `Neck -> Head` rotation
differs from `Neck2 -> Head` by up to **41.1 degrees**. This project uses the
true endpoint rotation.

### 3.3 Chain-composition identity

On the 18 recordings that carry the intermediate bones, the endpoint-relative
rotation was compared against the explicitly composed chain
(`Ab -> Spine2 -> Spine3 -> Spine4 -> Chest`, and `Neck -> Neck2 -> Head`).

36 checks, **worst error 3.735e-14 degrees**.

This confirms on real data that a parent-relative chain telescopes, so no chain
product needs to be computed and no error accumulates. The 51-bone recordings
cannot be checked this way because they lack the intermediates; for them, 3.1
provides independent validation instead.

## 4. Segmentation

The four workbooks were normalised into one tidy table,
`data/immutable/segmentation_normalized.csv`: **363 blocks** across 24
recordings.

* Schema drift: most sheets use `end_frame`; `671 - T1P1R1` uses
  `end_frame_exclusive` and is 35 rows x 25 columns with three duplicated
  column blocks and literal `'blank'` strings. Duplicates were collapsed to the
  first occurrence per exercise.
* All ends are normalised to exclusive (slice) semantics. Boundaries fall on
  whole-second multiples of 120 frames, so the inclusive/exclusive ambiguity is
  one frame (8.3 ms) and immaterial; it is normalised for reproducibility.
* 46 rows had an unmarked boundary. 45 are ex16/ex17, outside both the training
  (ex01-15) and evaluation (ex09-13) scope, and were dropped.
* One repair: `790-T1-R1` ex04/ex05 shared an unmarked boundary inside the
  training scope and was merged into a single contiguous 8 s block. This is
  sound because self-supervised training does not require exercise matching.

**Evaluation coverage is complete**: all five of ex09-13 are present in all 24
recordings, 50 to 57 seconds per recording.

Training pool, ex01-15 of T1: 582 s over 59 blocks for repetition 1, and 591 s
over 60 blocks for repetition 2, balanced across participants (141-151 s each).

## 5. Configuration corrections carried into this project

* `Chest_to_Neck` was assigned to both `head_neck` and `trunk_spine` in the
  source `body_regions.yaml`. Here every link belongs to exactly one of six
  regions.
* Feature manifests were renamed to their true link counts: the files named
  `14link` contain 18 links and those named `16link` contain 22.

## 6. Status

**S1 and S2 pass with no blocking issues.** Zero unexplained disagreements with
the trusted cache; the chain identity holds to 4e-14 degrees; all 24 recordings
yield the same 18 anatomical links.

Two findings must travel with every downstream result: the mid-study skeleton
template change for 651 and 671, and the 42-day versus 28-day follow-up for
participant 252.
