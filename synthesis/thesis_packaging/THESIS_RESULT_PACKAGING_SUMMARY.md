# Thesis result packaging summary

## One-sentence headline

Participant-specific longitudinal redistribution of body-link contributions to whole-body movement structure is detectable by JcvPCA relative to within-session R1/R2 variability, with selected complementary evidence of mixed regional coupling reorganization and other non-equivalent method layers — not a shared learning signature or formal A3 claim.

**651–ex13–`trunk_arm_lagged_coupling` INCREASE** (Δ≈+0.13 > Drep, same-sign both reps), in a participant–exercise context also highlighted by Conv and RQA. **671** shows mixed reorganization (ex12 trunk–arm ↑ ≈+0.20; ex10 regional ↓; ex09 stable despite strong JcvPCA).

## Strongest cross-method convergence

**651 D12:** JcvPCA A2 + strong Conv >Drep + entropy/dimensionality features + RQA complementary ex13 + trunk–arm coupling ↑ — complementary constructs, not identical measures. Incomplete anatomy (**4/10**).

---

## Strongest discordance

**671:** strongest JcvPCA vs sparse Conv. **790–ex11:** RQA complementary without robust coupling. **252 D12:** modest JcvPCA A2 vs Conv ≤Drep. **671–ex09:** JcvPCA redistribution with stable coupling.

## Strongest defensible adaptation claim

Findings are **consistent with** participant-specific longitudinal motor adaptation / **coordination reorganization** (selected changes exceed method-specific within-session repetition variability). **Not proven:** motor learning, DOF unfreezing, Gaga/psilocybin causality, universal coupling direction.
